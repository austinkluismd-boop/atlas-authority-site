Atlas Authority™

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 August 2026 · Contact: austinkluismd@gmail.com

Atlas Authority (“we”) operates a practice-operations platform for medical practices: website operations, measurement, and social-media management. This policy describes what the platform collects, why, and the choices available. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

Who the platform serves

Our users are the practices we work with and their authorized staff. The platform's marketing and measurement plane is designed to hold no patient identity: it has no fields for patient names, birthdates, or addresses, and clinical systems are outside it. Nothing in this policy changes that boundary.

What we collect

Account and access data. Names, work email addresses, and roles of the practice staff who are given access, and the access records their use creates (sign-ins, actions taken in the console).

Connected social and business accounts. When an authorized practice user connects a platform account (for example Google Business Profile, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, or X), we receive OAuth tokens and, through them, the profile information, content, and performance metrics those platforms make available for the connected account. We never receive or ask for the account’s password — sign-in happens on the platform’s own pages.

Website measurement. Aggregate search and website performance data for the practice’s own web properties (for example Google Search Console data).

How we use it

Solely to operate the platform for the practice that connected it: showing the practice its own performance, preparing and publishing content the practice approves, and keeping connections healthy. We do not sell data, we do not rent it, we do not use it for advertising networks, and we do not use data from one practice for another.

Google user data

Atlas Authority’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google account data reached through a connection (such as Business Profile or YouTube data) is used only to provide the connected practice’s own dashboards and publishing features, is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to operate the platform or as required by law, and is never used for advertising.

Storage and protection

OAuth tokens are stored server-side in an access-controlled vault, encrypted at rest, and are never exposed to browsers, logs, or third parties. Access to platform infrastructure is restricted to the platform operator.

Data deletion

Disconnect at any time. An authorized practice user can disconnect any platform account in the console (Connections → Disconnect). Disconnecting deletes the stored token for that account. Platform-side authorization can also be revoked in the platform’s own security settings (for example Google Account → Security → Third-party access, or Facebook Settings → Business Integrations).

Full deletion on request. To request deletion of all data associated with a connected account or a practice, email austinkluismd@gmail.com from an address associated with the practice. We confirm completion by reply.

Retention

Connection tokens live until disconnected or revoked. Performance measurements are retained for the practice’s own historical reporting and deleted on practice offboarding or verified deletion request, except where law requires longer.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted here with a new effective date.
